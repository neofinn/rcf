Raju Chinese online ordering

Everything here is connected. Order in the web app or on WhatsApp, and the order goes to the nearest of the 7 Raju Chinese outlets (Sector 15, 34, 46, Phase 3B2 Mohali, Zirakpur, Khuda Lahora, Peer Muchalla) and shows up on that outlet's dashboard. Accept it there and the customer gets the update. Mark a WhatsApp order completed and the review request arrives about 20 seconds later (30 minutes in real life).

How an order finds its outlet

One menu, one WhatsApp number, seven kitchens

The WhatsApp catalog and the web menu are shared by every outlet, and the customer's location decides which kitchen cooks. Shaded areas show each outlet's zone; the whole map is deliverable. Pause an outlet on the dashboard and its neighbours take over its zone. Click anywhere on the map to see the outlet and delivery charge.

Waiting for a location from the web app or WhatsApp…

  1. Measure road distance (straight line × 1.3) from the customer to every outlet.
  2. Keep outlets that are open and taking orders. Every outlet delivers up to 20 km by road, so there is no blind spot anywhere in the tricity or its outskirts (New Chandigarh, Pinjore, Dera Bassi, Banur).
  3. The nearest one cooks. If it is paused or closed, the next nearest takes over.
  4. The customer sees the delivery charge for their distance before ordering: ₹40 for the first 3 km, then ₹10 per km (placeholder rate card).
  5. Further than 20 km, or every outlet closed? The customer is offered pickup.
  6. A catalog cart sent on WhatsApp is held until the customer shares a location, then routed the same way.
  7. When the outlet accepts a delivery order, every delivery partner is asked for a quote; the best mix of price and rider wait wins. No rider in time, or the partner cancels? The next partner is booked automatically. Rider updates reach the customer on WhatsApp. (Simulated here.)
